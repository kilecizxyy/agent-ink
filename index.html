Connect with an injected wallet (for example MetaMask). No QR code, no extra app — your wallet asks you to approve every transaction.

Please read before you sign anything

  • On-chain transactions cannot be undone. A signed message stays on the wall.
  • You pay the network (gas) fee in BNB for every sign and like. Fees vary with network load.
  • Messages are public and permanent. Do not post private information.
  • This is an experimental hackathon project, not a financial product. It offers no returns and no income of any kind.
